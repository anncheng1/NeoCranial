    Measurements

    VI (Levene)
    Coronal at foramen of Monro: falx → lateral wall of lateral ventricle, each side
    AHW
    Same plane: diagonal width of frontal horn
    TOD
    Parasagittal: outer thalamus → tip of occipital horn
    3rd ventricle
    Coronal C3, transverse; normal < 3 mm
    TCD
    Mastoid view; ≈ GA in mm to 27 wk, then +2 mm/wk
    Cisterna magna
    Midline sagittal; normal 3–10 mm
